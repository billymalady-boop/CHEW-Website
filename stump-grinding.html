Stump Grinding

Ground down below the surface so the stump is gone for good — not just cut flush. Usually part of a clearing job, and standalone when there are enough stumps to make the trip cost-effective. Serving Coryell, Bell & Lampasas counties.

Gone for Good, Not Just Cut Flush

A leftover stump is a tripping hazard, a mower-killer, and a home for termites and rot. We grind stumps down below ground level so you're left with clean ground you can mow, plant, or build over — not a flush-cut stump that's still sitting there.

Usually Part of a Bigger Job

Most of our stump grinding happens as an add-on to a land clearing or forestry mulching job. The mulcher grinds most vegetation right down to — or below — ground level as it works. When terrain won't let the mulcher get a stump flush, our dedicated stump grinder finishes it off. If you're already having us clear or mulch, adding stump grinding to the same visit is the most cost-effective way to get it done.

Standalone Stump Grinding

We do take standalone stump-grinding jobs — when there are enough stumps to make the trip cost-effective. A minimum price applies, because getting the machine loaded, hauled to your property, and back is the same effort whether it's one stump or ten. Straight up: we won't come out for a single stump. If you've got a yard full of old stumps, a clearing job's worth of leftovers, or several trees that came down years ago, that's exactly the kind of standalone job that makes sense — call us and we'll take a look.

How CHEW Prices It

Stump grinding is quoted by stump size and count, and whether it's bundled with clearing or standing on its own. Bundled with a clearing or mulching job, it's an add-on to the whole. On its own, the job minimum applies. Text photos and a rough stump count to 737-235-1335 and we'll tell you straight whether it pencils out as a standalone or is better added to a clearing job.

Common Questions

Will you come out to grind just one stump?

Honestly, no — one stump doesn't cover the cost of loading and hauling the machine out. If you've got several stumps, or you're pairing it with a clearing job, that's when it makes sense. A minimum price applies to standalone stump work.

Can you grind stumps as part of clearing my land?

Yes — that's the most common way we do it. The mulcher takes most material to ground level, and the stump grinder finishes anything the terrain wouldn't let it reach.

How deep do you grind?

Below ground level, so the stump is gone for good and you're left with clean ground to mow, plant, or build over.

I have old stumps from years ago — can you do those?

Yes. Old fallen trees and leftover stumps are a good standalone job as long as there are enough of them to meet the minimum. Send photos and a count.

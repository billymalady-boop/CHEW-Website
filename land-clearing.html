Land Clearing in Central Texas

Take your land back. We clear overgrown, unusable acreage and hand it back to you open, walkable, and ready to build, graze, fence, or enjoy — across Coryell, Bell & Lampasas counties.

Land clearing in Central Texas means reclaiming overgrown, unusable acreage — cutting the cedar, brush, and small trees that have taken over so your property is open, walkable, and ready to build, graze, or fence again. CHEW clears land across Coryell, Bell & Lampasas counties.

Reclaim Land You Can't Even Walk

Cedar and brush creep in a little at a time, and one day you're paying taxes on acreage you can't graze, build on, hunt, or get to the back of. Land clearing is how you take it back. It's not one single technique — it's whatever mix of methods gets your property to the finish you actually want, done in a way that leaves the ground usable instead of scraped and eroded.

What You Can Do With Cleared Land

  • Build a homesite — open and level a spot for a house, barn, or shop.
  • Reclaim pasture — turn a cedar thicket back into grazeable, usable grass.
  • Open fence lines — clear the line so you can run, repair, or replace fence.
  • Get access — cut trails and lanes to reach the back of your property.
  • Reduce fire risk — thin the fuel load around structures and drives.
  • Just enjoy it — make land you're proud to stand on again.

The Right Method for Your Goal

We match the method to the outcome. Forestry mulching grinds everything into a soil-protecting mulch layer for a clean finish that slows regrowth. Brush cutting knocks growth down fast and affordably when you just need it opened up. For most Central Texas land — cedar, mesquite, brush, and small-to-mid trees — mulching is the workhorse, but we'll recommend what fits your property and budget at the site visit. Genuinely large hardwoods fall outside what the machine takes down, and we'll tell you that up front, not after we start.

Why It's Done Right the First Time

One machine, one operator who treats your land like his own. You get a written scope and price before we start, a confirmation before we roll out, and clean, usable ground when we leave. No ghosting, no surprise invoice. If something's not right, we own it and fix it.

How CHEW Prices It

Common Questions

Do you clear for homesites and building pads?

Yes. We clear and open the area, and can rough-grade the pad — see our site prep work. We'll shape it so it drains right and sits ready for what comes next.

Can you clear without taking down my oaks?

Yes — selective clearing is common. We take the cedar and brush and leave the mature oaks and pecans you want to keep. Mark or point them out at the walk and we work around them.

What about the cedar specifically?

Cedar (Ashe juniper) is the main thing we clear here. It has its own page — see cedar removal — because keeping it from coming back matters as much as the initial clearing.

How soon can you start?

Most projects schedule within a few days depending on workload and weather. Free estimate first, then a written quote you approve before anything happens.

LAND CLEARING & BRUSH REMOVAL
THE RECLAIM™
Land & Brush Clearing

Overgrown land isn't just an eyesore — it's a fire hazard, a predator habitat, and acres of your property you're paying taxes on but can't use. Cedar spreads fast in Central Texas. What's manageable today is a problem next year.

WHAT WE DO

We clear cedar, brush, overgrowth, fence lines, and pasture edges. Light vegetation to heavy density. We haul it off or mulch in place depending on your property and preference.

HOW IT WORKS

One call. We assess the property, quote by the acre or by the job, schedule the work, and leave it clean.

Debris Removal & Hauling

That pile has been sitting there longer than you want to admit. Demo debris, brush piles, junk, old lumber — it's not going anywhere on its own and every day it sits it's taking up space you could be using.

WHAT WE DO

We load it, haul it, and dispose of it properly. JCB handles the heavy lifting on machine jobs. Hand load on residential pickups. Dump trailer handles the haul. You handle nothing.

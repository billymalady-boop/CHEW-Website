Our Story

How CHEW Got Started

I'm a Central Texas landowner. I know firsthand how fast this land gets overgrown and unusable. I know how we collect things thinking they'll come in handy someday, until one day you've got a pile, a barn or garage you can't use, and junk scattered across your property. I have a quarter-mile gravel drive that needs constant attention. I had old structures sitting there, a liability I kept ignoring, until I found rodents and rattlesnakes moving in.

Property is like a vehicle. Ignore it long enough and it costs you more than it ever should have.

So I bought a skid steer, got the attachments, and cleaned my place up. Then a friend asked for help. Then a neighbor. I found out I loved the work. When I retired, CHEW was the natural next step.

But there was another reason. Over the years I hired a lot of contractors. A few were excellent. Most weren't. They'd show up late, work a few hours, drag the job out, and ask for more money. Some did decent work and left their trash blowing across my property. I got tired of it.

I knew I could do better, not because I'm perfect, but because I commit, I show up, and I do the work. When mistakes happen, and they will, I own them and I fix them. That's what CHEW is built on.

Why Choose CHEW?
Because we show up. That sounds simple until you've called three contractors who never called back, or watched someone take a deposit and disappear. I'm a Central Texas landowner, one of you, and I built CHEW because I got tired of the same bad experiences you've had. Right equipment, right work ethic, and a crew that's there when we said we'd be. We commit, we show, we do.
What Makes CHEW Different?
One call, one crew, one machine. Whether you need brush cleared, a driveway fixed, debris hauled, or a site prepped, we handle it start to finish. No surprises on the invoice. Just the work done right and the property left clean.

Services
Can you fix potholes and washouts?
Yes. We repair potholes, washouts, ruts, and related driveway damage. Many driveways can be restored by regrading existing material, saving you money on new gravel.
Can you deliver and spread material?
Yes. We deliver it, spread it, or both. Already have material on site? We'll spread it. No need to source it through us.
Do I need to pile up my debris before you arrive?
No. We handle gathering, loading, hauling, and disposal. Leave it where it is.
What types of debris do you remove?
Construction debris, brush piles, old sheds, fencing, storm debris, estate and property cleanouts, storage unit cleanouts, and general junk hauling. Hazardous materials are excluded and flagged upfront.
Can you clear brush and small trees?
Yes. We clear small cedars, saplings, heavy brush, and overgrowth. Large tree removal is outside our current scope and we'll tell you upfront.
Can you mow overgrown fields and pastures?
Yes. We have a mega mower capable of reclaiming overgrown fields, pastures, and heavily vegetated areas.
Do you tear down old sheds and structures?
Yes. We demolish and remove sheds, playsets, and small outbuildings, then haul everything off and leave the site clean.
Do I need a permit for demolition?
Permit requirements vary by location. We recommend checking with your county before work begins.

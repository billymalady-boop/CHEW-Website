Gravel Driveways

Repair and installation done right — base prep, grading, crown, and compaction so water sheds off instead of cutting ruts. Serving Coryell, Bell & Lampasas counties.

A Driveway That Sheds Water and Lasts

A washed-out driveway beats up your vehicles and tells everyone your property isn't kept up. The fix is almost never "dump more gravel" — it's shape and drainage. We rebuild the crown so water runs to the sides, fix the base, and compact it so it sets up firm instead of turning into next season's potholes. Done right, the driveway holds up, sheds rain, and makes the whole place look cared for.

Repair or New Install

  • Repair / regrade — potholes, ruts, and washouts. Often we can reshape the gravel that's already been pushed into the shoulders and save you money on new material.
  • Base rebuild — when the rock is gone or the base has gone soft, we rebuild it and re-crown.
  • New install — bare-dirt two-tracks, new building sites, and fresh access roads built from the ground up.

We Handle the Material, Too

You don't have to coordinate a gravel delivery or figure out tonnage. We arrange the material and handle all the spreading, grading, and compaction with our equipment — you just get a finished driveway. (For the full how-and-why, see our gravel driveway repair guide.)

How CHEW Prices It

Every driveway is quoted as a whole after we see it — length, width, how bad the base is, drainage, and how much new material it actually needs all factor in. You get one clear written number before any work starts, and a minimum job price applies. Text a few photos to 737-235-1335 for a rough range.

Common Questions

Can you fix potholes and washouts without all-new gravel?

Often yes. Many driveways can be restored by regrading existing material and rebuilding the crown — that saves you the cost of a full load of new rock.

What kind of gravel do you use?

For a driveway that locks together and holds, we use a crushed base material with fines (commonly called crusher run or road base), with a topping stone over it if you want the look. The base is what carries the weight.

Do you build new driveways and ranch roads?

Yes — from bare dirt. We can also keep a long ranch drive on a maintenance regrade so it doesn't wash out every year.

How long does a driveway take?

Most are a one-day job depending on length and how much base work and material are involved. We'll tell you at the estimate.

How Much Land Can You Clear in a Day?

July 2026 · 6 min read · Serving Coryell, Bell & Lampasas counties

A skid-steer forestry mulcher clears roughly half an acre to two acres per day in Central Texas, depending on brush density, tree size, and terrain. Light cedar and brush go fast; heavy, mature stands with big trunks slow it down. Rocky caliche and steep ground pull the rate down further.

The Honest Range

Anyone who quotes you a flat "acres per day" without seeing the land is guessing. The real range for forestry mulching runs from about half an acre to two acres in a working day. Two properties that are both "five acres" can take wildly different amounts of time — the acreage barely tells the story. What's growing on it, and how big, is what matters.

What Speeds the Work Up

Light, uniform growth is fast: young cedar, brush, tall weeds, and saplings mulch quickly and let the machine move steadily. Flat, dry, open ground with good access means less repositioning and no waiting on soft spots. A property that's overgrown but not heavily timbered is where you get the most acres cleared in a day.

What Slows It Down

Four things eat the clock. Density — a wall of mature cedar takes far more passes than scattered brush. Tree size — big trunks near the top of what the mulcher can handle grind slowly, and anything oversized has to be handled differently. Terrain — our caliche, rock outcrops, and slopes force a careful, slower pace. Cleanup standard — grinding everything to a fine, even mulch takes longer than a rough knockdown.

Light vs. Heavy: Two Real Examples

An acre of scattered brush and young cedar on flat ground might be a morning's work. An acre of dense, mature cedar with big trunks on rocky, rolling terrain — ground down clean — can be most of a day on its own. Same acre on paper, very different jobs in the field.

Why This Matters for Your Quote

Because time in the field is the real cost driver, we quote every job as a whole after a site visit or a good look at your photos, with a minimum job price — not a blind per-acre rate that punishes you or shorts us. Send a few photos of the thickest and the lightest parts of your property and we can usually give you a realistic ballpark before we ever drive out. More on how land clearing is priced here.

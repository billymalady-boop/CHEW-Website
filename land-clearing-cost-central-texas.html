What Does Land Clearing Cost in Central Texas?

July 2026 · 7 min read · Serving Coryell, Bell & Lampasas counties

Land clearing in Central Texas is priced by the job, not a flat per-acre rate — because brush density, tree size, terrain, access, and cleanup method change the work dramatically. Light brush costs far less than heavy, mature cedar. Every job is quoted as a whole after a site visit, and a minimum job price applies.

Why There's No Flat Per-Acre Price

The honest answer landowners deserve: a real per-acre number doesn't exist until someone sees your land. Any per-acre figure you find online is a ballpark at best — two five-acre tracts can differ by thousands of dollars depending entirely on what's growing and where. We quote the whole job after a site visit or a good look at your photos, and a minimum job price applies to cover getting the equipment out and back.

The Factors That Move Your Price

Brush density is the biggest one — scattered brush mulches fast; a solid wall of cedar takes many more passes. Tree size matters because large trunks grind slowly and anything oversized needs a different approach. Terrain — our caliche, rock, and slopes slow equipment and add wear. Access — a tight gate, soft ground, or a long haul to the work area adds time. Cleanup standard — a fine, even mulch finish takes longer than a rough knockdown. Time in the field is the real cost, and these factors set the time.

Mulching vs. Haul-Off

How the material is dealt with swings the price. Forestry mulching grinds everything in place and leaves it as a mulch layer — no hauling, no dump fees, usually the most cost-effective route. Traditional clearing that pushes, piles, and hauls material off-site costs more because of the extra equipment, passes, and disposal. For most overgrown-land jobs, mulching gives you the better result for less.

How Our Quoting Works

We keep it simple and honest. Send photos of your property — especially the thickest and the lightest areas — and we can often give a realistic ballpark before driving out. For anything sizable we'll do a free on-site assessment and quote the job as a whole, so you know the number before we start. No blind per-acre rate, no surprise change orders for conditions we could have seen up front.

Ways to Keep the Cost Down

A few things genuinely help your budget: clearing in the winter dormant season when the work goes cleaner, choosing mulching over haul-off when you don't need bare dirt, prioritizing the areas you actually use instead of the whole property at once, and staying ahead of regrowth so cedar and brush never get back to a full reclaim job.

Best Time of Year to Clear Land in Central Texas

July 2026 · 6 min read · Serving Coryell, Bell & Lampasas counties

You can clear land in Central Texas year-round — and the best time is usually as soon as the overgrowth is costing you usable land, access, or fire safety. Every season works; each just carries minor trade-offs. Cedar and most brush can be mulched any month, so there is rarely a reason to wait.

The Short Answer

There is no season you have to wait for — we clear year-round, and the right time is whenever the brush is costing you land you could be using. Each season has small trade-offs worth knowing, but none of them are a reason to let an overgrown property sit.

What Winter Offers

Cooler months have a few advantages if your schedule is flexible. Deciduous brush and grasses go dormant, so there is less green material and sightlines open up, making it easier to see and work around what you are keeping. The ground is typically firmer and drier than in spring, so equipment moves cleanly without rutting. And the snakes, ticks, and wasps that make summer work miserable are mostly gone. All nice if you can time it — but none of it is a reason to hold off if your land needs clearing now.

Summer Clearing: What to Know

Land can be cleared in summer — it just comes with trade-offs. Central Texas heat is hard on crew and equipment, fire risk climbs during dry, windy stretches (and burn bans can affect debris options), and everything's green and grown-in, so a job can look bigger than it is. It's still very doable; you just want a contractor paying attention to fire conditions and working accordingly.

Cedar Is a Year-Round Job

Ashe juniper (cedar) is evergreen, so it doesn't have a dormant season the way brush does — you can mulch it any month of the year. Many landowners tackle cedar in winter anyway to fold it into a larger clearing project and take advantage of the better working conditions. If cedar's your main problem, timing is flexible.

Don't Wait on a Season

We work year-round, so the best time to start is whenever you are ready — there is no reason to let the property sit waiting on the calendar. Demand does spike in spring when everything greens up and folks see how far it has gotten away from them, so if you have a project in mind, get on the books early either way. Send photos whenever you are ready and we will take a look.
